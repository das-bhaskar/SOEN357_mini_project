Health Companion

A multi-generational bridge for medication safety and family care.

The Project Vision

Managing chronic illness is rarely a solitary task; it is a family effort. Health Companion is a mobile solution designed to solve the friction between caregivers managing complex households and seniors striving for medical independence.

This case study documents the design of an ecosystem that balances high-speed data retrieval for crisis moments with high-accessibility interfaces for daily maintenance. By utilizing a Warm Sunset color palette, we move away from clinical, intimidating medical UIs toward a supportive, human-centric experience.

Intentional Inclusive Design

A core challenge identified in our research was the "Dexterity Gap." Traditional medical apps often use small, dense text and tiny buttons that are difficult for senior users to navigate.

Why the "Big Buttons"?

  • Accessibility for Rajesh (Senior): To accommodate tremors or reduced motor control, the interface utilizes oversized touch targets to prevent "fat-finger" errors.
  • Emergency Clarity for Sunita (Caretaker): In high-stress asthma or diabetic emergencies, bold icons and vibrant colors allow for split-second decision-making without the need to read dense text.
  • Gamified Safety: The "playful" high-contrast aesthetic is a deliberate choice to reduce the psychological stress and anxiety often associated with chronic disease management.

What to Expect

The following report details a 9-step UX journey focusing on two primary use cases:

  • The Caretaker Perspective (Sunita): A centralized dashboard for managing multiple dependents, featuring biometric FaceID entry and a rapid "Emergency Records Vault" for asthma and diabetes.
  • The Senior Perspective (Rajesh): An "Error-Proof" interface designed for dexterity and visual support, utilizing 3-second tactile logging and high-contrast accessibility.
