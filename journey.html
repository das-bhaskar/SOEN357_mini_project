User Journeys

A deep dive into the user experience lifecycle.

Sunita's Journey

Scenario: Emergency school call during work hours.

GOAL:

Find & Share Son's Asthma Plan ASAP

STAGES
01 Trigger
02 Access
03 Switch
04 Retrieve
05 Share
DOING

Receives urgent call from school nurse while in a meeting.

Excuses herself and opens the Health Companion app.

Taps the "Household" toggle and selects her son's profile.

Navigates to "Vault" and clicks "Emergency Plans".

Hits share and sends the PDF directly to the school nurse.

THINKING

"I hope I can find the latest dosage chart quickly."

"Please let the FaceID work on the first try."

"I'm glad I don't have to log out of my own account."

"Is this the 2026 version or the old one?"

"Done. Now I can get back to the meeting."

FEELING
Panic
Anxious
Relieved
Focused
Calm
PAIN POINTS

Stress of interrupting work.

Biometric lag or password friction.

Confusion if profiles aren't distinct.

Information overload in the vault.

Email app switching overhead.

Rajesh's Journey

Scenario: Morning insulin dose and blood sugar check.

GOAL:

Log Morning Vitals with Independence

STAGES
01 Awake
02 Remind
03 Log
04 Report
05 Confirm
DOING

Wakes up at 7:00 AM. Joints feel stiff and vision is blurry.

Hears the high-volume audio reminder from the app.

Opens the app to log blood sugar and confirm insulin dose.

Uses voice input to record a note about "stiff fingers."

Receives a large, bright confirmation screen.

THINKING

"I hope I can handle the phone without dropping it today."

"I'm glad that sound is loud enough to hear from the nightstand."

"The buttons are big enough that I don't need my glasses yet."

"I'll just tell the app how I feel instead of typing."

"I feel safe knowing Sunita can see I've taken it."

FEELING
Stiff
Alert
Confident
Empowered
Peaceful
PAIN POINTS

Osteoarthritis makes grip strength weak in the morning.

Low-contrast alerts are impossible to read at 7 AM.

Small "X" or "Submit" buttons cause mis-taps.

Typing is painful for arthritic knuckles.

Anxiety about "forgetting" if the dose was logged.

Insight for Design

For Rajesh, **Independence** is the core emotional driver. If the UI is too "small" or "minimalist," he feels old and incapable. By using **High-Contrast Focus Mode** and **Giant Elements**, we restore his dignity and autonomy.
