Wireframe Logic Summary

The logic follows the blue arrow transitions in the wireframe: Sunita moves from biometric entry to a centralized board, then drills down into Arav's records for crisis sharing. Conversely, Rajesh's flow is linear and high-contrast, utilizing a 3-second hold to ensure medication like Insulin (20mg) is logged confidently without technical errors.
