User Research

Evidence-based insights from 12 participants. (Family and Friends)

How I did the Research

I started by looking at reviews of currently medical apps to understand the common problems people face with medical apps. After that, I conducted a survey with 12 participants by calling my family and friends to get real-world feedback.

What stood out: Most people weren't just frustrated with one thing; they were overwhelmed by juggling different tasks. The seniors I spoke to specifically pointed out that buttons are often too small, while the parents in the group mentioned how annoying it is to switch between their own health info and their kids' info. I used these specific 'pain points' to decide exactly how the app should look and work.

Key Insight: Senior Barrier

100% of participants over 60 reported "Visual Fatigue" and "Medication Anxiety." This data justifies Rajesh’s persona and the need for simplified, high-contrast UI.

Key Insight: Portal Fatigue

Caregivers highlighted "Toggling multiple profiles" as their primary stressor. This directly informed Sunita’s persona and the multi-user "Super App" logic.
