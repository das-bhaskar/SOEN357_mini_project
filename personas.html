User Personas

Deep-dive into the behaviors and needs of our primary users.

NAME Sunita
AGE 45
TECH LEVEL
OCCUPATION

Senior Software Engineer

"I’ve only got a few minutes between meetings—if the app can't show me everyone's status in one click, it's useless to me."

Background

Sunita is part of the "sandwich generation," managing her own thyroid condition while acting as the primary health administrator for her asthmatic son and aging father. She works in a high-pressure tech role where her day is dictated by tight delivery deadlines.

Goals

  • Unified household management overview.
  • Rapid info retrieval during school emergencies.
  • Sync medical tasks with work calendars.

Challenges

  • Mental drain from context switching.
  • Portal fatigue across different providers.
  • Zero tolerance for complex onboarding.

Core Needs

One-Tap Profile Switcher: A prominent toggle for "Sunita," "Son," and "Father."

Shared Access: Real-time status sharing with her husband and school nurse.

NAME Rajesh
AGE 68
TECH LEVEL
OCCUPATION

Retired Civil Servant

"I don't want to bother my daughter for every pill. I just need buttons big enough to see and hit without my glasses."

Background

A retired professional living with Type 2 Diabetes and Osteoarthritis. While tech-literate for basic tasks, he is terrified of "breaking the app" and struggles with the tiny buttons and thin fonts common in modern minimalist design.

Goals

  • Maintain medical independence.
  • Fail-safe dose confirmation system.
  • Simple symptom updates to his doctor.

Challenges

  • Arthritis makes small touch targets impossible.
  • Declining vision makes low contrast unreadable.
  • Anxiety over data loss or "wrong clicks."

Core Needs

High-Contrast Focus Mode: Bold dark backgrounds with mint/blue text.

Giant Action Buttons: Elements at least 60px tall for stiff fingers.

Voice Input: Record quick symptom notes instead of typing.
